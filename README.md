
# 🍫 North America Wonka Bar Chocolate Sales Analysis (2020–2024)


## 📌 Project Overview
This project delivers a comprehensive **business intelligence dashboard** analyzing Wonka Bar chocolate sales across North America from 2020–2024.  
It highlights **orders, sales, profit, shipment modes, seasonal demand, and city-level performance** to support strategic decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Identify top-selling chocolate products driving order volumes.
- Compare profitability across regions to uncover strong and weak markets.
- Analyze shipment mode distribution for logistics optimization.
- Track profit growth trends over multiple years.
- Compare monthly order values to identify peak and declining phases.
- Pinpoint top-performing cities for targeted campaigns and expansion.

## 📈 Analytics Approach (SMART Framework)

### 1. Descriptive Analytics
- **Products**: Milk Chocolate dominates demand; mid-range items (Fudge Mallows, Nutty Crunch) show moderate traction.  
- **Regions**: Pacific leads with $30.49K profit; Gulf lags at $14.7K.  
- **Shipment**: Standard Class dominates (~60% orders).  
- **Yearly Profit**: Stable at ~$20K (2021–22), sharp rise to $30K+ in 2023–24.  
- **Seasonality**: November & December peak demand months.  
- **Cities**: New York leads with ~1000 orders; top 3 cities contribute 40%+ demand.  

### 2. Diagnostic Analytics
- Heavy reliance on **Milk Chocolate** and **Pacific region**.  
- Gulf region and weaker products underperform due to poor positioning/distribution.  
- Shipment preference shows cost-driven customer behavior (Standard Class).  
- Seasonal spikes reveal dependence on festive demand.  
- Low-order cities (Columbia, Jackson, Richmond) suffer from weak visibility.  

### 3. Predictive Analytics
- Profits projected to reach **$36K–40K by 2026** if momentum continues.  
- Top chocolates expected to contribute **60–70% of profits**.  
- Gulf region may grow **10–15% yearly** with corrective strategies.  
- Same Day shipment adoption could rise to **10–12% annually** if pricing improves.  
- Seasonal peaks (Nov–Dec) will continue dominating unless demand diversifies.  

### 4. Prescriptive Analytics
- **Products**: Sustain top chocolates; bundle/promote mid-range; reposition weaker items.  
- **Regions**: Invest in Pacific; strengthen Atlantic & Interior; corrective action in Gulf.  
- **Shipment**: Maintain Standard Class; optimize Second Class; expand First Class loyalty; improve Same Day pricing/availability.  
- **Seasonality**: Maximize festive months with inventory & promotions; boost off-season with targeted campaigns.  
- **Cities**: Loyalty programs in New York; promotions in LA & Philadelphia; local marketing/logistics in mid-tier cities; awareness campaigns in weaker cities.

## ✅ Conclusion
The Wonka Bar business is profitable with **$93.4K profit (2020–24)** and steady growth.  
- **Top performers**: Milk Chocolate, Pacific region, New York City.  
- **Seasonal drivers**: November & December.  
- **Preferred logistics**: Standard Class shipment.  

**Strategic focus**: Sustain leaders, strengthen mid-tier, uplift weaker regions/cities, and diversify demand for balanced long-term growth.
